# Troubleshooting Guide

## Purpose

No deployment platform is perfect.

During the development of this project a number of real engineering problems were encountered and resolved. This document records those issues, explains how they were investigated and documents the final solutions.

The goal is not only to provide operational guidance but also to demonstrate a structured troubleshooting methodology expected of a Platform Engineer.
